About InfraNova

Infrastructure
reimagined.

InfraNova is an AI-powered smart infrastructure monitoring system designed to improve road safety and emergency response. ESP32-CAM devices installed on roadside poles capture images at regular intervals, while AI detects problems such as open manholes, potholes, road cracks, and accidents. When an issue is detected, the system sends an alert to a central dashboard so authorities can respond quickly.
